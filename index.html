Sketch narrative

  • Context: This clock is designed for focused productivity contexts like studying, deep work, or reading, where constantly checking a phone can be distracting. It provides a calm, ambient sense of time passing without demanding attention or interrupting concentration.
  • Design decisions: I chose a candle metaphor because it represents time as something that slowly depletes, which aligns with how focused work often feels. Hours are encoded as the physical burn level of the candle, reinforced by a reusable vertical hour scale beside it, allowing the clock to function continuously rather than requiring reset pins. Minutes are represented through wax drips, where each drip corresponds to five minutes, providing coarse but readable progress without encouraging minute-by-minute checking. Seconds are encoded through a gentle, rhythmic flame sway that completes one full back-and-forth motion per second, making the visualization feel alive while remaining subtle enough not to distract. The neutral color palette and minimal layout were chosen to reduce visual noise and support sustained attention.
  • Future work: This clock could be extended with light interaction, such as clicking the candle to start a timed focus session or adjusting the burn rate to match Pomodoro-style intervals. Another extension would be adding multiple candles to represent parallel tasks or longer-term goals across a day.
